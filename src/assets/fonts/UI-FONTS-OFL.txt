Included as unmodified subsets (woff2). The Arabic subsets were produced by
Google Fonts; the Latin subsets are the upstream @fontsource 5.3.0 latin
subsets, copied unmodified.

Roles are kept separate and are not interchangeable:

  Aref Ruqaa            Arabic display headings only.
  IBM Plex Sans Arabic  Arabic UI and body text.
  IBM Plex Sans         Latin UI and body text.
  IBM Plex Serif        Latin display only — route titles and the featured
                        headline. Never body.

Plex Sans and Plex Sans Arabic are siblings in one superfamily, which is why
the pair was chosen: this app switches between English and Arabic at a toggle,
and the two scripts have to sit at the same optical weight on the same row.

None of the above is ever used to render Qur'anic text. Mushaf text renders
only through the bundled official KFGQPC face for its own riwayah — Hafs v0.18
or Warsh v10 — see KFGQPC-NOTICE.txt. The ayah medallion is an ornament rather
than Qur'anic text and is the single permitted exception, using Amiri Quran.
