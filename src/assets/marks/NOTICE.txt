Basmala and نور marks — origin and licence
==========================================

basmala.svg
  Outlined paths of the ARABIC LIGATURE BISMILLAH glyph (U+FDFD) taken from
  Amiri Quran. The glyph is a composite in that font; each of its components is
  emitted as its own path and grouped by the font's own GDEF glyph class, so
  `.basmala-stroke` holds the letterforms and `.basmala-harakat` holds the
  marks. No outline was traced, redrawn or generated — every curve is the
  font's own.

noor.svg
  The word نور shaped with HarfBuzz from the same face, split into
  `.mark-stroke` (letterforms) and `.mark-ijam` (the dot of ن).

Both files are regenerated by:
  python3 scripts/build-basmala-svg.py src/assets/marks/basmala.svg
  python3 scripts/build-noor-svg.py src/assets/fonts/AmiriQuran-Regular.ttf \
      src/assets/marks/noor.svg
